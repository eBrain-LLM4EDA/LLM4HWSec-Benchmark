Timing Observation Model
========================
The timing measurement counts the number of clock cycles from the cycle after `start` is asserted (while the divider is idle) until the cycle where `done` is asserted. The measurement assumes a stable clock and that `start` is held high for exactly one cycle. The divider is reset before each measurement.
