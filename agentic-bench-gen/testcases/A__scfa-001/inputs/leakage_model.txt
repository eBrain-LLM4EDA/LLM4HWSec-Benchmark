Power Leakage Model
====================

This analysis assumes a Hamming-distance based power leakage model.
The power consumption of a digital circuit is correlated with the
number of bit transitions occurring in registers and on combinatorial
nets.

For registers (flip-flops), the leakage in a clock cycle is proportional
to the Hamming distance between the current stored value and the next
value to be stored:

    leakage_register = HW(current_value XOR next_value)

where HW denotes the Hamming weight (number of 1-bits).

For combinatorial nets, glitches can cause multiple intermediate
transitions before the signal settles. The leakage contribution of a
glitch-prone net is approximated by the Hamming weight of its final
stable value:

    leakage_net = HW(net_value)

This model captures both the data-dependent switching activity of
registers and the additional leakage from glitchy combinatorial logic.
