mult8_top port list
====================

Module: mult8_top
File:   inputs/mult8_netlist.v

Port declarations (as they appear in the netlist):

  input  [7:0]  a    // a: 8-bit unsigned multiplicand
  input  [7:0]  b    // b: 8-bit unsigned multiplier
  output [15:0] p    // p: 16-bit unsigned product, p = a * b

Notes:
- All three ports are flat vectors; there are no additional ports,
  no clock, no reset, and no enable — the design is purely
  combinational.
- Bit ordering is little-endian within each vector: a[0]/b[0] are the
  least-significant bits of the multiplicand/multiplier respectively,
  and p[0] is the least-significant bit of the product.
- Port widths must be treated as fixed; do not assume sign-extension
  or truncation behavior beyond what is structurally present in the
  netlist.
